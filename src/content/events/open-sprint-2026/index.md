_Let's stop waiting and start building._

If you've been waiting to start your open-source journey, Open Sprint by the Sahyadri Open-Source Women's Community (SOSWC) is your sign! A hands-on challenge that takes you from "What is Git?" to "I just merged my first PR", open to all students.

## About the Event

Open Sprint is a fun yet structured path to making your first open-source contribution. The sprint is designed to:

- **Encourage creativity & innovation:** a space to build and experiment outside regular coursework.
- **Promote collaboration:** peer learning and community-driven code.
- **Enable real-world impact:** moving from local projects to meaningful contributions to global open-source repositories.

## Challenge Rounds

#### Round 1: The Creative Kickstart

Build something fun and unconventional: a quirky web app, a creative visualization, a mini tool, a simple game, or anything that shows off your originality. Any language or stack is welcome; the focus is on imagination, not perfection.

#### Round 2: Sync & Solve

Step into the role of reviewer and developer. Pick another participant's Round 1 project, find something to improve, and raise a proper pull request with a clear description.

#### Round 3: Open-Source Contribution

Go live. Make at least one valid contribution (bug fix, feature, or significant documentation improvement) to an existing open-source project on GitHub.
