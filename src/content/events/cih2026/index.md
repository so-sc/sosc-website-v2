A national-level, 24-hour hackathon bringing together innovators, problem-solvers, and aspiring entrepreneurs from across the country, at Sahyadri Campus on **11th and 12th July 2026**.

## About the Event

CIH 2026 is the flagship national-level hackathon organized by BuildUp Kasaragod in collaboration with Sahyadri College of Engineering & Management and the Sahyadri Open Source Community. Teams take on real-world problem statements across healthcare, fintech, and smart cities, and build working prototypes in a 24-hour nonstop sprint on campus.

For registration and updates, visit the [CIH 2026 Website](https://cih.cis2026.com/).

## Innovation Themes

#### 1. Healthcare & Well-Being

Develop a real-time system that monitors workforce stress, predicts staffing bottlenecks, and supports department-specific resource allocation in public hospitals.

#### 2. Fintech & Financial Inclusion

Build an AI-powered fintech platform that leverages alternative data to provide dynamic credit scoring, instant micro-loans, and automated insurance payouts for underserved workers and rural entrepreneurs.

#### 3. Smart & Sustainable Cities

Develop a real-time maritime emergency response system that enables instant distress alerts, coordinated rescue dispatch, and seamless communication between distressed vessels, rescue teams, and onshore authorities.

#### 4. Open Innovation

Develop innovative solutions that leverage technology to solve community challenges, enhance public services, and create meaningful social impact.

## Eligibility & Teams

- Open to all undergraduate degree college students, in any year of study.
- Teams of up to **4 members**, all from the same institute.
- An institute can send any number of teams, but each participant can be part of only one team.
- All members of a shortlisted team must be present for the offline round.
- Certificates are awarded only to participants who attend on campus.
