The SOSC Podcast brings listeners closer to the minds behind the community. Recorded during DevHost 2025, its episodes feature conversations with founding members and industry experts.

Each episode explores technical journeys, leadership lessons, and the evolution of open-source initiatives: a look at how innovation, mentorship, and collaboration drive SOSC's impact in the tech ecosystem.
