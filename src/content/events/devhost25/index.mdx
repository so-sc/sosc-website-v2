_Connecting Minds, Crafting Progress._

Three days of dev talks, hands-on masterclasses, competitions, and a 36-hour hackathon, all under one roof at Sahyadri Campus, Adyar, from **6th to 8th November 2025**.

## About the Event

DevHost, the annual flagship event of the Sahyadri Open Source Community, returns with its 8th edition at Sahyadri College of Engineering and Management, Adyar, Mangalore. This year brings an exciting mix of dev talks, hands-on workshops, and masterclasses in domains like Web3, AI agents, and APIs, delivered by industry experts. Panel discussions highlight opportunities in Mangalore's growing IT ecosystem and explore upcoming technology trends.

DevHost embodies SOSC's mission of cultivating a culture of innovation and collaboration, promoting open-source values like transparency and inclusivity, and bridging the gap between academic learning and real-world application.

### At a Glance

- **6+** talks, mentoring sessions, and masterclasses
- **12+** distinguished speakers and **50+** industry experts
- **70+** colleges and **2000+** students from around the state
- **36-hour** hackathon with a **₹60,000+** prize pool
- Official event certificates, DevHost merchandise, and recruitment opportunities

## DevHack 2025

Compete on industry-relevant problem statements, collaborate with peers, and receive expert mentorship throughout the event. Stand a chance to win from a grand prize pool of **₹60,000** in cash and rewards.

- **Round 1 (Online Ideation):** Teams submit idea presentations (PPT) on chosen themes.
- **Round 2 (Onsite Finals):** Shortlisted teams race to solve a new set of problem statements at Sahyadri College, Mangalore.
