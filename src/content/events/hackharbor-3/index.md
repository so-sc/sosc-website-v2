HackHarbor is an SOSC-led peer-to-peer learning initiative for first-year students preparing to enter their second year.

HackHarbor 3.0 focused on strengthening fundamentals, exposing participants to real development workflows, and encouraging collaborative learning through guided sessions led by senior students. The programme created a supportive environment where juniors learned by building, asked questions freely, and grew alongside their peers.
