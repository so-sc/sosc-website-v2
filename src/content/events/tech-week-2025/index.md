Tech Week was a series of sessions on trending tech topics and practical workflows, led by SOSC community members across March and April 2025.

## Sessions

- **Serverless Unleashed**
- **Tux101: Linux for Everyone**
- **GCP Accelerate**
- **Self-Hosting Essentials**
- **GoFr Workshop**

Attendees gained hands-on experience, practical insights, and guidance on modern development practices. The week fostered collaboration, curiosity, and skill-building, highlighting SOSC's role in bringing open-source knowledge to students.
